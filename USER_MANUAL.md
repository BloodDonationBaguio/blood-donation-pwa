## System Overview

The Blood Donation System is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to streamline the blood donation process for donors and administrators. The system allows users to:

- Register as blood donors
- Track donation applications
- View donation history
- Check eligibility status
- Monitor blood inventory levels

**Key Features:**
- Secure user authentication
- Online donor registration
- Real-time application tracking
- Donation history management
- Mobile-responsive design
- Progressive Web App (PWA) capabilities

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

To use the Blood Donation System, you need:

- **Device:** Desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone
- **Browser:** Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- **Internet:** Stable internet connection
- **Email:** Valid email address for account creation and notifications

### Accessing the System

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the system URL (provided by your blood donation center)
3. The homepage will display with options to register, login, or learn about donation

## User Registration

### Creating Your Account

1. **Navigate to Sign Up**
   - Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage
   - Or go directly to the signup page

2. **Fill in Your Information**
   - **Full Name:** Enter your complete legal name
   - **Email Address:** Use a valid email you check regularly
   - **Password:** Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password for verification

3. **Complete Registration**
   - Review your information for accuracy
   - Click the "Sign Up" button
   - Check your email for verification (if required)

### Password Security Tips

- Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers and special characters
- Avoid using personal information (birth dates, names)
- Don't reuse passwords from other accounts

## Login and Account Access

### Logging In

1. **Go to the Login Page**
   - Click "Login" on the homepage

2. **Enter Your Credentials**

3. **Access Your Dashboard**

If you forget your password:

1. Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page

4. Follow the link to create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

### Security Features

- Sessions automatically expire after 1 hour of inactivity
- Secure password storage with encryption
- Protection against unauthorized access
- Session management for multiple device security

## Donor Registration Process

### Starting Your Donation Application

1. **Log In** to your account
2. Click "Donor Registration" or "Donate Now"
3. Complete the comprehensive donor form

### Personal Information Required

**Basic Details:**
- First Name and Last Name
- Gender
- Date of Birth (must be 18-65 years old)
- Contact Information (email, phone)

**Physical Information:**
- Weight (minimum 50 kg / 110 lbs)
- Height
- Blood Type (if known)

**Location Information:**
- Complete Address
- City/Municipality
- Province
- Postal Code

### Eligibility Questions

The system will ask about:

- Recent illnesses or medical conditions
- Current medications
- Recent surgeries or medical procedures
- Travel history (certain countries may restrict eligibility)
- Lifestyle factors (tattoos, piercings, etc.)

### Submission Process

1. **Review Your Information**
   - Double-check all entered data
   - Ensure contact information is accurate

2. **Submit Application**
   - Click "Submit Registration"
   - Wait for confirmation message
   - Save your reference number for tracking

3. **Receive Reference Number**
   - A unique reference number (format: DNR-XXXXXX)
   - Email confirmation with details
   - SMS notification (if enabled)

## Dashboard and Donation History

### Accessing Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your personalized dashboard with:

- **Welcome Message:** Personalized greeting with your name
- **Quick Actions:** Donate Now, Track Application buttons
- **Donation History:** Complete record of your donations
- **Eligibility Status:** When you can donate again

### Understanding Donation History

Your donation history displays:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | Date of donation or application |
| Blood Type | Your blood type (A+, B-, O+, etc.) |
| Status | Current status of your application/donation |

### Status Meanings

- **Donation Completed:** Successfully donated blood
- **Approved:** Application approved, awaiting donation
- **Pending:** Application under review
- **Rejected:** Application not approved (with reason)

### Eligibility Information

The system tracks your donation eligibility:

- **90-Day Rule:** Must wait 90 days between donations
- **Next Eligible Date:** Shows when you can donate again
- **Days Remaining:** Countdown until next eligible donation

## Tracking Your Application

### Using the Tracking System

You can track your application status without logging in:

1. **Go to Track Page**
   - Click "Track Application" from the homepage
   - Or navigate directly to the track page

2. **Enter Reference Number**
   - Input your DNR-XXXXXX reference number
   - Click "Track" to view status

3. **View Application Status**
   - Current status of your application
   - Date of last update
   - Additional notes or requirements

### Reference Number Format

Your reference number follows this format:
- **Prefix:** DNR (Donor Registration)
- **Code:** 6-character alphanumeric code
- **Example:** DNR-1A2B3C

### Status Updates Available

- **Submitted:** Application received and under review
- **Under Review:** Being processed by medical staff
- **Approved:** Eligible to donate
- **Scheduled:** Donation appointment set
- **Completed:** Successfully donated
- **Rejected:** Not eligible (with specific reason)

## Eligibility Requirements

### Basic Eligibility Criteria

**Age Requirements:**
- Minimum: 18 years old
- Maximum: 65 years old

**Weight Requirements:**
- Minimum: 50 kg (110 lbs)
- No maximum weight limit

**Health Requirements:**
- Good general health
- No acute illness
- No recent infections
- Stable chronic conditions may be acceptable

### Temporary Deferral Periods

You may be temporarily unable to donate if you've had:

- **Recent Surgery:** Wait 6-12 months
- **Tattoos/Piercings:** Wait 6-12 months
- **Dental Work:** Wait 24 hours for simple procedures
- **Travel:** Certain countries require waiting periods
- **Pregnancy:** Wait 6 months after delivery
- **Medications:** Some require waiting periods

### Permanent Deferral Conditions

Certain conditions permanently prevent donation:

- HIV/AIDS
- Hepatitis B or C
- Certain types of cancer
- Heart disease
- Bleeding disorders
- Organ transplant recipients

### Documentation Required

For first-time donors, you may need:
- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of address
- Medical history information

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How often can I donate blood?**
A: You can donate whole blood every 90 days (approximately 3 months).

**Q: How long does the donation process take?**
A: The entire process takes about 30-45 minutes, including registration, screening, and donation.

**Q: Is donating blood safe?**
A: Yes, all equipment is sterile and single-use. The process is safe and regulated.

**Q: What should I eat before donating?**
A: Eat a regular meal and drink plenty of water. Avoid fatty foods.

### Account and Technical Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and follow the email instructions.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, you can update your email in your profile settings or contact support.

**Q: Is my personal information secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures.

**Q: Can I use the system on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes, the system is mobile-responsive and works on all devices.

### Donation Process Questions

**Q: What happens after I submit my application?**
A: Your application is reviewed by medical staff, and you'll receive updates via email.

**Q: How will I know if I'm approved to donate?**
A: You'll receive an email notification and can check your status online.

**Q: What if I'm not eligible to donate?**
A: You'll receive information about why and when you might become eligible.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Login Issues

**Problem:** Can't log in with correct credentials
**Solutions:**
- Check that Caps Lock is off
- Verify email spelling
- Reset your password if needed
- Clear browser cache and cookies

**Problem:** Account locked or session expired
**Solutions:**
- Wait 15 minutes and try again
- Clear your browser data
- Contact support if issues persist

### Registration Issues

**Problem:** Form won't submit
**Solutions:**
- Check all required fields are filled
- Verify email format is correct
- Ensure password meets requirements
- Try a different browser

**Problem:** Reference number not working
**Solutions:**
- Double-check the reference number format
- Wait 24 hours after registration
- Contact support with your details

### Technical Issues

**Problem:** Page not loading or errors
**Solutions:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser

**Problem:** Mobile app issues
**Solutions:**
- Update your browser
- Check device storage
- Restart your device
- Ensure good internet connection

### Browser Compatibility

**Recommended Browsers:**
- Chrome (latest version)
- Firefox (latest version)
- Safari (latest version)
- Edge (latest version)

**Unsupported Features:**
- Internet Explorer
- Very old browser versions
- Some mobile browsers may have limited functionality
