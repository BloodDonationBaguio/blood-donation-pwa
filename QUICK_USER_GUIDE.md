# BLOOD DONATION SYSTEM
# Quick User Guide

## Complete User Journey - Step by Step

### Step 1: Open Website
🌐 **Access the System**
- Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Navigate to: `https://your-blood-donation-site.com`
- Wait for the homepage to load completely

---

### Step 2: Click Sign Up
📝 **Create Your Account**
- Look for the **"Sign Up"** button on the homepage
- Click it to begin registration
- You'll be redirected to the account creation page

---

### Step 3: Verify Email
✉️ **Email Verification**
- Fill in your personal details:
  - Full Name
  - Email Address
  - Password (minimum 8 characters)
  - Confirm Password
- Click **"Sign Up"**
- Check your email inbox for verification message
- Click the verification link in the email
- If you don't see the email, check your spam folder

---

### Step 4: Log In
🔑 **Access Your Account**
- Return to the website
- Click **"Login"** button
- Enter your email and password
- Click **"Login"** to access your dashboard
- You'll see a personalized welcome message

---

### Step 5: Click Donate Now
🩸 **Start Donation Process**
- On your dashboard, find the **"Donate Now"** button
- Click it to begin the donor registration process
- This will take you to the comprehensive donor form

---

### Step 6: Fill Donor Form
📋 **Complete Your Information**

**Personal Information:**
- First Name and Last Name
- Gender (Male/Female/Other)
- Date of Birth (must be 18-65 years old)
- Weight (minimum 50 kg / 110 lbs)
- Height
- Blood Type (if known)

**Contact Information:**
- Email Address (pre-filled from your account)
- Phone Number

**Address Information:**
- Complete Street Address
- City/Municipality
- Province
- Postal Code

**Medical Screening:**
- Answer all health-related questions honestly
- Declare any medications you're taking
- Disclose recent surgeries or medical procedures
- Mention recent travel history if applicable

---

### Step 7: Submit Application
✅ **Complete Registration**
- Review all entered information for accuracy
- Ensure all required fields are completed
- Click **"Submit Application"** button
- Wait for confirmation message

---

### Step 8: Save Reference No.
📄 **Record Your Reference**
- You'll receive a unique reference number (format: DNR-XXXXXX)
- **Save this number** - you'll need it for tracking
- Reference number is also sent to your email
- Example: `DNR-1A2B3C`

---

### Step 9: Track Application
🔍 **Monitor Your Status**
- Go to the **"Track Application"** page
- Enter your reference number (DNR-XXXXXX)
- Click **"Track"** to view current status
- Check status regularly for updates

**Status Meanings:**
- 🟡 **Pending** - Application under review
- 🔵 **Under Review** - Being processed by medical staff
- 🟢 **Approved** - Eligible to donate
- 🔴 **Rejected** - Not eligible (with reason provided)

---

### Step 10: Wait for Approval
⏰ **Application Processing**
- Processing typically takes 2-5 business days
- You'll receive email notifications for status changes
- Check your tracking page for updates
- Contact support if no update after 7 days

---

### Step 11: Visit Center
🏥 **Schedule Your Donation**
- Once approved, visit the donation center
- **Bring required documents:**
  - Valid government-issued ID
  - Your reference number
  - List of current medications (if any)

**Preparation Before Visit:**
- Eat a healthy meal 2-3 hours before
- Drink plenty of water
- Get a good night's sleep
- Avoid alcohol 24 hours before
- No fatty foods on donation day

---

### Step 12: Donate Blood
🩸 **Complete Your Donation**
- **Process Duration:** 30-45 minutes total
- **Actual Donation:** 8-10 minutes
- **Post-Donation:** Rest for 15 minutes with refreshments

**During Donation:**
- Relax and breathe normally
- Inform staff if you feel unwell
- You can save up to 3 lives with one donation!

**After Donation:**
- Rest for 15 minutes
- Enjoy provided refreshments
- Avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours
- Drink plenty of water
- You can donate again after 90 days

---

## 📞 Quick Help & Support

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Can't find verification email?**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Add support@blooddonation.system to contacts
- Request new verification email

**Forgot password?**
- Click "Forgot Password?" on login page
- Enter your email address
- Follow reset link in email

**Reference number not working?**
- Double-check the format (DNR-XXXXXX)
- Wait 24 hours after registration
- Contact support with your details

**Application taking too long?**
- Normal processing: 2-5 business days
- Check your email for updates
- Call support if delayed beyond 7 days
